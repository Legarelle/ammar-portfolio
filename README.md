### 2. 3D Curved Spatial Project Gallery (`+2,050m`)

* **Cylindrical 3D Ring (`preserve-3d`)**: Portrait cards arranged along a curved 3D arc with custom SVG sculptural headers.

* **Hover-to-Pause & 3D Enlarge**: Hovering over any card pauses orbital rotation, pulls the card forward in Z-space (`+95px`), flattens its perspective angle toward the viewer, and dims sibling cards.

* **Category Filtering & Drag-to-Spin**: Filter dynamically by `All (6)`, `AI & ML`, `Full-Stack & Cloud`, or `3D & Real-Time`, or drag horizontally to spin the gallery ring.
